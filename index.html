Voice effects

These change your voice while you sing. They aren't sound effects for the song.

Tap a pad to turn it on or off. Hold and slide up for more, down for less, then let go to lock it. Presets: tap an empty one to save the pads you have on. Tap a saved one to load it; while it's selected, every change you make is saved to it. Tap it again to stop, or hold it to replace it.

Note for the AI

    Music vibe

    The key comes from your voice. To pick one yourself, write it here, like "in A minor".

    These are used as style hints: tempo, sounds and mood. The app can't add an artist's actual songs, recordings or voice.

    Music you like (reference)

    From a file, the app measures tempo, brightness and energy and matches your song to them. It can't open links: with Claude on, the link text is passed along as a hint. Nothing from the reference is copied into your song.
