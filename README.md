# Sing to Song

Record your voice with live effect pads, cut and layer your takes, pin notes for the AI, and turn it into a finished song with a visualizer video.

**Use it:** https://aleksandriax.github.io/sing-to-song/

The whole app is one file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser over https (or from your own computer) and allow the microphone.

## What's in it

- **Step 1 – Record & cut:** a record button (tap, or hold and release), 12 adjustable voice-effect pads with presets, and one waveform with up to 4 layers. Split, duplicate and delete right on the wave.
- **Step 2 – Notes & vibe:** scroll the song, pin notes (with durations) for the AI, describe the vibe, name artists you like, add a reference song. The key, tempo and chords come from your voice.
- **Step 3 – Listen & save:** play, regenerate, add sound effects, save audio or a visualizer video, save drafts.
- **Discover and Profile:** creators, songs and sound effects, following, a profile photo, privacy and selling controls, Premium and a verified-badge request.

## Preview limits

Everything is saved in the browser on the device you use. Accounts across devices, seeing other people's real uploads, real payments (Premium and sound-effect sales, with the app's 15% fee) and real verification need a server that isn't built yet; in the app those parts are marked as previews or test purchases. The creators marked "Example" are made up.
